Full terms and refunds →
How do I pay?+

Through PayPal. You can pay with your PayPal balance or any debit or credit card, and you don't need a PayPal account to pay by card.

Can I pay part now and part later?+

Yes. For Business and Store you can pay half of the build to start and the other half when the site is ready to launch. Anything else on your order, like a discovery, an audit or a monthly plan, is paid in full.

What if I'm not sure which package I need?+

Send me a message before you order. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll point you to the right one, or put together a custom price.

Who owns the website?+

You do. The site, the content and the code are yours once the final payment is in. I build on your hosting and your accounts wherever I can.

What if I'm not happy?+

Each package comes with rounds of changes, and I keep going until it matches what we agreed. If I can't deliver what we agreed, you get your money back for the work not done.

When are monthly plans charged?+

SEO and Meta Ads are paid in advance, so their first month is part of your payment on the day you order. The care plan is paid after each month of work. From then on, all your monthly plans are one PayPal subscription, charged on the same day each month, starting one month after you order.

I found you on Fiverr. Can I order here?+

If we first met on Fiverr, please order through Fiverr, so we both stay within its rules. This page is for clients who find me another way: through a referral, search or this site.

Do monthly plans have a contract?+

No. They renew each month and you can cancel whenever you like, from your PayPal account or by telling me.

Is the ad budget included in Meta Ads monthly?+

No. Your ad budget goes straight to Meta on your own card. My fee covers setting up, running and reporting on the campaigns.
